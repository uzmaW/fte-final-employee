## 📋 How AI Employee Tracks Progress

The AI Employee will:

1. **Weekly Monitoring**
   - Check current MRR progress vs. targets
   - Monitor hiring pipeline
   - Track feature development status
   - Review customer satisfaction metrics

2. **Weekly Reporting**
   - Update Dashboard.md with progress
   - Generate weekly metrics summary
   - Alert on KPIs at risk (red/yellow status)
   - Recommend corrective actions

3. **Monthly Reporting**
   - Generate comprehensive monthly report
   - Compare actuals vs. targets
   - Explain variances
   - Update next month targets if needed

4. **Quarterly Reporting**
   - Compile quarterly CEO briefing
   - Highlight key achievements
   - Document learning and recommendations
   - Prepare materials for board review
